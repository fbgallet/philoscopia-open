# Neurath's boat

*Argument for [Justification of belief](../axes/epistemic-justification.md) : Coherentism*

**We repair our knowledge like sailors rebuilding their ship on the open sea, plank by plank, never able to put it in dry dock.**

*[Neurath](../philosophers/neurath.md), [Quine](../philosophers/quine.md), 1932*

## The argument

1. No belief comes alone: a perception, a testimony, a theory count only by fitting with the rest of what we know. An observation that contradicts everything else is suspected first: an optical illusion, a measurement error.
2. The supposed foundations are themselves revisable: perception errs, yesterday's evidence falls, and we correct them by leaning on the rest of the web.
3. What justifies a belief, then, is its place in a whole whose parts support one another, like the planks of a ship, not a base set out of the water.

**⇒** So justification is a matter of **coherence**: a belief holds by the support of the others, and knowledge advances by repairing itself from within.
