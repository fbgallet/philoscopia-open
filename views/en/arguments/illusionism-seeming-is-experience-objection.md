# The objection from seeming

*Objection to [The enigma of consciousness](../axes/consciousness-nature.md) : Illusionism — attacks the position*

**An illusion is still something undergone: to say experience is mere appearance is to reassert it while believing one denies it.**

*[Strawson](../philosophers/strawson.md), *"The Consciousness Deniers"*, 2018*

## The argument

1. An **illusion** is what seems otherwise than it is: the stick seems broken in water, it is not. The concept therefore always presupposes two terms, a seeming and a being, and the gap between them.
2. But in the case of experience, the seeming is precisely what is at issue: that pain seems painful to me already is the pain. There is no room for a gap between suffering and its seeming to be suffering.
3. To say that the brain [represents](../glossary/representation.md) itself as having an experience it does not have is thus to describe a state in which something is present to someone: precisely what was to be eliminated.
4. Everything else can be granted: that introspection deceives us about the richness of the visual field, about our motives, about the nature of our states. Those errors bear on the **description** of experience, and they presuppose that there is something to describe badly.

**⇒** Illusionism can correct the idea we form of experience; it cannot cancel it, for the illusion it invokes is already a form of it.

## Ways out of the objection

- **Experience is undeniable, and the hard problem with it: it is the one fact I cannot doubt.**
- **Experience is real and cerebral: it is our conception of qualia that is false, not their existence.** : This is the road of non-eliminativist [physicalism](../axes/consciousness-nature.md): keeping the experiences, dropping the private, ineffable properties ascribed to them.
- **The objection takes for a fact what is a representation: that the seeming seems indubitable is precisely the effect to be explained.** : [Dennett](../philosophers/dennett.md)'s reply: the assurance with which we refuse to let go of qualia is part of the phenomenon, not a demonstration of it.
