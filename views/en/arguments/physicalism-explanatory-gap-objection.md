# The explanatory gap objection

*Objection to [The enigma of consciousness](../axes/consciousness-nature.md) : Physicalism — attacks the reasoning*

**That experience depends on the brain and acts through it, no one disputes; but the step from dependence to identity has not been taken.**

*Levine, *"Materialism and Qualia: The Explanatory Gap"*, 1983*

## The argument

1. The argument establishes at best that mental and cerebral states are **correlated** and occupy the same place in the chain of causes. Yet a correlation, however perfect, is not yet an **identity**: two inseparable things may still be two, and one may hold that the mental [supervenes](../glossary/supervenience.md) on the cerebral without concluding that they are one.
2. In science's successful identifications, identity comes with an explanation: once molecular agitation is understood, one sees **why** that amounts to heat, and one can no longer conceive of heat without it.
3. Nothing of the sort here: knowing everything about the circuitry of pain, the question "but why does it hurt?" stays open and perfectly intelligible. An **explanatory gap** remains that no other scientific identification has left behind it.
4. The causal argument cannot decide between the two possible readings of that gap, provisional or final: it treats experience as a causal link, whereas it is precisely its qualitative face, the [qualia](../glossary/qualia.md), that raises the problem.

**⇒** The argument proves that experience is moored to the brain, not that it reduces to it: physicalism here remains a promise, the promise that one day the gap will close.

## Ways out of the objection

- **The gap is not provisional: physical description states structures and functions, never what it is like to live them.** : You join [property dualism](../axes/consciousness-nature.md): the brain stays physical, but it bears properties its description does not contain.
- **If matter described from outside can contain nothing of the sort, perhaps it has been described too poorly: it has an inside.** : This is [panpsychism](../axes/consciousness-nature.md)'s wager: closing the gap not by adding something to matter, but by ceasing to believe it purely outward.
- **Every identification first seemed inconceivable: the gap is a state of our ignorance, not a discovery about the world.**
