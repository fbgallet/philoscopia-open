# Neo-Confucianism

*1017 – 1584, living tradition*

Neo-Confucianism is the great renewal of Confucian thought under the Song and Ming dynasties (eleventh to sixteenth centuries). Facing [Buddhism](../movements/buddhism.md) and [Taoism](../movements/taoism.md), which dominated metaphysical reflection, thinkers such as Zhou Dunyi, Zhang Zai and the Cheng brothers gave [Confucian](../movements/confucianism.md) ethics a cosmology founded on the pair of [principle](../glossary/li-principle.md) ([li](../glossary/li-principle.md)) and [vital force](../glossary/qi.md) ([qi](../glossary/qi.md)); they polemicized against these rivals while borrowing questions and practices from them. [Zhu Xi](../philosophers/zhu-xi.md) synthesized it and fixed the canon of the Four Books, which would form the basis of the imperial examinations until 1905. Lu Jiuyuan and later [Wang Yangming](../philosophers/wang-yangming.md) countered with a "school of mind" centred on [innate moral knowledge](../glossary/liangzhi.md) ([liangzhi](../glossary/liangzhi.md)). This debate runs through the tradition, which found in Korea, with Yi Hwang and Yi I, its last great renewers recorded here, spread to Japan, was read by [Leibniz](../philosophers/leibniz.md) and revived in the twentieth century as "New Confucianism".

## Core positions

1. [Where the sacred resides](../axes/transcendence.md) : **The ultimate principle is not outside the world: it dwells in each thing and is lived in human relations.**
2. [Vision of humanity](../axes/human-vision.md) : **Human nature is good: evil is only the obscuring of a principle everyone carries within.**
3. [The point of philosophy](../axes/metaphilosophy.md) : **Anyone can become a [sage](../glossary/the-sage.md), through study and through a practice in which knowing and acting are one.**

## Positions

### [Where the sacred resides](../axes/transcendence.md)

*positioned · explicit in the texts · major*

Immanence 85% · Transcendence 15%

[Principle](../glossary/li-principle.md) does not lie in a beyond: it is wholly present in each thing, according to the doctrine that principle is one and its manifestations many (li yi fen shu). Against Buddhism, accused of taking the world as illusory and of fleeing human relations, the Neo-Confucians assert that ultimate truth lies in the duties of children, parents and subjects.

*Sources : Zhu Xi and Lü Zuqian, Reflections on Things at Hand (Jinsilu), I and XIII · Zhang Zai, The Western Inscription (Ximing)*

### [Vision of humanity](../axes/human-vision.md)

*positioned · explicit in the texts · major*

Anthropological optimism 85% · Anthropological pessimism 15%

In the line of [Mencius](../philosophers/mencius.md), human nature is good, for it is nothing other than principle within us. Evil comes from the turbid vital force that obscures it and from the selfish desires grafted onto it, hence the need for study and effort. [Xunzi](../philosophers/xunzi.md), who held human nature to be bad, was excluded from the orthodox transmission.

*Sources : Zhu Xi, Commentary on the Mencius, 6A · Cheng Yi, in the Jinsilu, I*

### [The point of philosophy](../axes/metaphilosophy.md)

*positioned · explicit in the texts · major*

Transforming oneself 80% · Understanding the world 20%

The aim of study is to become a [sage](../glossary/the-sage.md), and that aim is open to all. [Zhu Xi](../philosophers/zhu-xi.md) leads there through the [investigation of things](../glossary/gewu.md) ([gewu](../glossary/gewu.md)), the methodical reading of the classics and the practice of quiet sitting (jingzuo). [Wang Yangming](../philosophers/wang-yangming.md) leads there through the [unity of knowing and acting](../glossary/unity-of-knowing-and-acting.md): to know the good without doing it is not yet to know it.

*Sources : Zhu Xi, Classified Conversations (Zhuzi yulei), books 7-14 · Wang Yangming, Instructions for Practical Living (Chuanxilu), I.5*

### [Relation to desire](../axes/desire.md)

*positioned · explicit in the texts · major*

Reasoned selection 60% · Extinction and detachment 35% · Affirmation 5%

The maxim calling for preserving [heavenly principle](../glossary/li-principle.md) ([tianli](../glossary/li-principle.md)) and extinguishing human desires targets selfish desires, not natural needs, which themselves belong to principle. It would earn Neo-Confucianism reproaches for its severity, notably from Dai Zhen in the eighteenth century.

*Sources : Zhu Xi, Zhuzi yulei, book 13 · Dai Zhen, Evidential Commentary on the Meanings of Terms in the Mencius*

### [Partiality](../axes/partiality.md)

*positioned · explicit in the texts · minor*

Partialism 85% · Impartialism 15%

[Humaneness](../glossary/ren.md) ([ren](../glossary/ren.md)) makes the noble person one body with heaven, earth and the ten thousand things, but it is exercised by degrees, from those near to those far. Neo-Confucianism upholds [graded love](../glossary/graded-love.md) against the impartial concern of the [Mohists](../movements/mohism.md).

*Sources : Cheng Hao, On Understanding Humaneness (Shiren pian) · Wang Yangming, Inquiry on the Great Learning (Daxue wen)*

## Influenced by

- **[Buddhism](../movements/buddhism.md)** (reacts against) : [Neo-Confucianism](../movements/neo-confucianism.md) defined itself against [Buddhism](../movements/buddhism.md), accused of taking the world as empty and of fleeing family and political duties. Yet it borrowed Buddhism's questions about mind, nature and meditation, to which it gave Confucian answers.
- **[Confucianism](../movements/confucianism.md)** (reinterprets) : [Neo-Confucianism](../movements/neo-confucianism.md) refounded [Confucianism](../movements/confucianism.md) around the Four Books, the Mencius among them, and a line of transmission of the Way (daotong) that leaps over the centuries to the Cheng brothers; the classics received a metaphysical reading they did not have before.
- **[Taoism](../movements/taoism.md)** (reinterprets) : The Diagram of the Supreme Ultimate of Zhou Dunyi, which came out of [Taoist](../movements/taoism.md) circles, became the cosmology of [Neo-Confucianism](../movements/neo-confucianism.md): the "Ultimateless" and yin-yang were put in the service of a Confucian ethics. The diagram's Taoist origin, reported by tradition, was held against Zhu Xi by his opponents.

## Influence on

- **[Leibniz](../philosophers/leibniz.md)** (reinterprets) : [Leibniz](../philosophers/leibniz.md) read [Zhu Xi](../philosophers/zhu-xi.md)'s [li](../glossary/li-principle.md) through the treatises of the missionaries Longobardo and Sainte-Marie, who saw materialism in it, and recognized in it instead a natural theology close to his own, proof in his eyes that reason can reach God without revelation.
