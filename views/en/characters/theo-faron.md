# Theo Faron

*Character from « Children of Men » (Alfonso Cuarón, 2006)*

A former activist turned civil servant in London, Theo moves through a childless world with a whisky and a weary irony. His ex-wife Julian asks him to help Kee, a young refugee, get out of the country. He agrees at first for money. The film follows him step by step without lending him a conversion or a speech: it is through his gestures that he moves from indifference to caring for a person.

**Practiced** : The principle of hope 68% · Lucidity without hope 33%

At first Theo makes a show of believing in nothing: even if a cure were found, he says in substance, it would be too late. He never professes any hope. Yet he keeps going with Kee at every point where he could give up, (Spoilers : until he rows, wounded, toward the meeting point of a ship that may never come). What he says places him on the side of [lucidity without hope](../axes/hope.md); what he does belongs to a hope without optimism, one that acts without certainty about the outcome.
