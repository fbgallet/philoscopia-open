# Power or Impotence of Subjectivity?

*[Hans Jonas](../philosophers/jonas.md) · 1981 · *Macht oder Ohnmacht der Subjektivität?**

An essay in which [Jonas](../philosophers/jonas.md) attacks [epiphenomenalism](../glossary/epiphenomenalism.md): he argues that the mind really acts on the body without violating the laws of physics, a condition without which no responsibility would be possible. It extends *[The Imperative of Responsibility](../works/imperative-of-responsibility-jonas.md)* into the philosophy of mind.
