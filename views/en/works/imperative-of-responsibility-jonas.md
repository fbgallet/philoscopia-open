# The Imperative of Responsibility

*[Hans Jonas](../philosophers/jonas.md) · 1979 · *Das Prinzip Verantwortung**

Subtitled "An Ethics for the Technological Civilization", [Jonas](../philosophers/jonas.md)'s major work, written in German. Because technology has changed the nature of action, he formulates a new imperative toward [future generations](../axes/future-generations.md), grounds duty in being and proposes a [heuristics of fear](../glossary/heuristics-of-fear.md). It closes with a critique of utopia.
