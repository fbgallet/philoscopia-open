# Principia Mathematica

*[Russell](../philosophers/russell.md) · 1910*

Written with [Whitehead](../philosophers/whitehead.md) in three volumes, these Principia set out to derive the whole of arithmetic from logic alone, with no appeal to intuition. The undertaking, which the paradox of sets Russell discovered forced him to complicate, gave modern logic its symbolic apparatus and set the style of [analytic philosophy](../movements/analytic-philosophy.md) for a century.
