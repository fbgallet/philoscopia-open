# On the Mode of Existence of Technical Objects

*[Gilbert Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md) · 1958*

The complementary thesis defended and published in 1958. Against a culture that misunderstands machines, [Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md) describes the genesis of technical objects, their [concretization](../glossary/concretization.md) and their [associated milieu](../glossary/associated-milieu.md), then the human relation to machines, which he wants to be that of an organizer and interpreter rather than a master or a slave.
