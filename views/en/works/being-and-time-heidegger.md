# Being and Time

*[Martin Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md) · 1927 · *Sein und Zeit**

The great treatise, left unfinished, in which [Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md) raises anew the question of the meaning of being through an analysis of human existence, [Dasein](../glossary/dasein.md): [being-in-the-world](../glossary/being-in-the-world.md), [care](../glossary/sorge.md), the "[they](../glossary/das-man.md)", [being-toward-death](../glossary/being-toward-death.md) and temporality. Only the first part appeared; the book marked the whole of twentieth-century philosophy.
