# Letter on Humanism

*[Martin Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md) · 1947 · *Brief über den Humanismus**

Written in 1946 in reply to the philosopher Jean Beaufret and published in 1947, this letter distances itself from [Sartre](../philosophers/sartre.md)'s existentialism and makes the human being not the lord of beings but the "shepherd of being", whose "house" is language. It marks [Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md)'s entry into post-war French philosophical debate.
