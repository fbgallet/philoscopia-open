# The Question Concerning Technology

*[Martin Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md) · 1954 · *Die Frage nach der Technik**

A lecture given in Munich in 1953 and published in 1954 in *[Lectures and Essays](../works/essays-and-lectures-heidegger.md)*, growing out of a cycle given in Bremen in 1949. [Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md) argues there that the essence of modern technology is nothing technological: it is a mode of revealing, [enframing](../glossary/gestell.md), which challenges everything to stand by as available.
