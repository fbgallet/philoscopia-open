# Technik, Medizin und Ethik

*[Hans Jonas](../philosophers/jonas.md) · 1985 · *Technik, Medizin und Ethik. Zur Praxis des Prinzips Verantwortung**

A collection in which [Jonas](../philosophers/jonas.md) applies *[The Imperative of Responsibility](../works/imperative-of-responsibility-jonas.md)* to medicine and biotechnology: human experimentation, the definition of death, the right to die, genetic engineering and cloning, against which he invokes a "right to ignorance".
