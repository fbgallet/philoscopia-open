# Marriage and Morals

*[Russell](../philosophers/russell.md) · 1929*

A historical and polemical inquiry into sexual morality: Russell argues that the doctrine which makes sexual pleasure a sin has produced an amount of shame, ignorance and cruelty that no good outweighs, and asks that conduct be judged by its effects. The book cost him, in 1940, an appointment at the City College of New York, annulled by court order before he could take it up, and weighed heavily in his Nobel Prize in literature.
