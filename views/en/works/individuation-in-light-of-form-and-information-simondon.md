# Individuation in Light of Notions of Form and Information

*[Gilbert Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md) · 2005*

The principal thesis, defended in 1958, first published in two partial volumes (L'Individu et sa genèse physico-biologique, 1964; L'Individuation psychique et collective, 1989, the year of the author's death), then in full in 2005. [Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md) criticizes substantialism and the hylomorphic schema, and thinks the individual from [individuation](../glossary/individuation.md): from a [preindividual](../glossary/preindividual.md) and [metastable](../glossary/metastability.md) being arise the physical individual, the living, the psychic and the [transindividual](../glossary/transindividual.md).
