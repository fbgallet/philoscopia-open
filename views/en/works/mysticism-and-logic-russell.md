# Mysticism and Logic

*[Russell](../philosophers/russell.md) · 1918*

A collection Russell assembled and published himself in 1918, gathering ten essays that had appeared between 1902 and 1914. On one side the technical pieces that ground his method: "On Scientific Method in Philosophy", "On the Notion of Cause", which dismisses causality in favour of functional dependence, and "Knowledge by Acquaintance and Knowledge by Description". On the other the essays written for a general readership, chief among them "A Free Man's Worship" (ch. III), the most widely read of all, and the title essay, which grants the mystical feeling real value while denying it any cognitive authority.
