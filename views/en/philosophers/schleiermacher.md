# Schleiermacher

*1768 – 1834 · German · [Romanticism](../movements/romanticism.md)*

German theologian and philosopher close to the Jena Romantic circle, translator of Plato: he made hermeneutics a general art of understanding, and religion a feeling of absolute dependence.

## Influence on

- **[Philosophical hermeneutics](../movements/hermeneutics.md)** (reacts against) : [Schleiermacher](../philosophers/schleiermacher.md) founded general hermeneutics, whose aim is to understand the author as well as and better than he understood himself. [Philosophical hermeneutics](../movements/hermeneutics.md) keeps its scope but rejects this aim: the meaning of a text is not its author's intention.
