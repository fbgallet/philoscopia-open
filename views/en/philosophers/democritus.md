# Democritus of Abdera

*460 BCE – 370 BCE · Greek*

With Leucippus, he holds that only atoms, indivisible bodies, and the void in which they move exist; sweet, bitter or colour exist only "by convention". His atomism will pass to [Epicurus](../philosophers/epicurus.md).

- **[Epicurus](../philosophers/epicurus.md)** (reinterprets) : [Epicurus](../philosophers/epicurus.md) takes over [Democritus](../philosophers/democritus.md)'s physics, atoms moving in the void, but adds the swerve of the atoms ([Lucretius](../philosophers/lucretius.md)'s *clinamen*), which makes room for freedom, and puts it in the service of an ethics of tranquillity of mind.
