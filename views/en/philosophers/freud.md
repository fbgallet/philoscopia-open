# Sigmund Freud

*1856 – 1939 · Austrian*

Founder of psychoanalysis, who held that the self is not master in its own house: unconscious desires and motives govern part of our thoughts and acts.

## Influenced by

- **[Schopenhauer](../philosophers/schopenhauer.md)** (reinterprets) : [Freud](../philosophers/freud.md) finds in [Schopenhauer](../philosophers/schopenhauer.md), whom he says he read little, the intuition of a blind will beneath clear consciousness: the primacy of drive, repression, and sexuality as an obscure force are sensed there before psychoanalysis brings them to light.

## Influence on

- **[Frankfurt School](../movements/frankfurt-school.md)** (synthesizes) : From the 1930s the Frankfurt Institute combined psychoanalysis with social critique to understand why the masses consent to their domination. In Eros and Civilization, [Marcuse](../philosophers/marcuse.md) drew from it a reading of [Freud](../philosophers/freud.md) in which the repression of drives becomes a historical fact.
