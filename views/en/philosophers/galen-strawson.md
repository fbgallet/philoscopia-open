# Galen Strawson

*1952 – ? · British*

Contemporary British philosopher known for his argument against ultimate moral responsibility: since no one is the cause of themselves, no one is the ultimate author of their acts.

## Influenced by

- **[Russell](../philosophers/russell.md)** (reinterprets) : What is taken up is an argument, not a doctrine: physics gives us only the structure of things, never their intrinsic nature, and experience is the one portion of the world we know otherwise than from outside. [Strawson](../philosophers/galen-strawson.md) exploits that vacant place in a direction [Russell](../philosophers/russell.md) did not take: he peoples it with experiential properties, as far as panpsychism, where Russell held the stuff to be [neutral](../glossary/neutral-monism.md), that is, precisely neither mental nor physical. The label 'Russellian monism' covers both and blurs the gap: [one and the same pole](../axes/consciousness-nature.md) houses a thesis of agnosticism about the stuff and a thesis about its nature.
