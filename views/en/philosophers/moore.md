# Moore

*1873 – 1958 · English · [Analytic Philosophy](../movements/analytic-philosophy.md)*

English philosopher, [Russell](../philosophers/russell.md)'s colleague at Cambridge and one of the sources of analytic philosophy: he defends common sense against idealism and holds that goodness is a simple, indefinable quality that every attempt to reduce it to a natural fact misses.
