# Abū Ḥāmid al-Ghazālī

*1058 – 1111 · Persian · [Kalām](../movements/kalam.md)*

Theologian and mystic, author of The Incoherence of the Philosophers, the most famous critique of [falsafa](../movements/falsafa.md), and of an account of his own crisis of doubt.

## Influenced by

- **[Falsafa](../movements/falsafa.md)** (reacts against) : [Al-Ghazālī](../philosophers/al-ghazali.md) refutes twenty theses of the [falsafa](../movements/falsafa.md) philosophers, notably the eternity of the world and the necessity of the causal link, and declares three of them incompatible with Islam: the eternity of the world, divine knowledge restricted to universals and the denial of bodily resurrection.
