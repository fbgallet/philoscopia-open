# Nicolas Malebranche

*1638 – 1715 · French · [Rationalism](../movements/rationalism.md)*

French philosopher and Oratorian priest, a critical disciple of [Descartes](../philosophers/descartes.md): for him no creature truly acts, God alone is a cause, and the events of the world are only the occasions of his action. He also held that we see all things in God.
