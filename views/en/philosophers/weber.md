# Max Weber

*1864 – 1920 · German*

German sociologist and political thinker: he defines the modern state by its monopoly on legitimate physical violence, and opposes the ethics of conviction to the ethics of responsibility, which owns the consequences of a measured use of force.

## Influenced by

- **[Nietzsche](../philosophers/nietzsche.md)** (reinterprets) : [Weber](../philosophers/weber.md), for whom a thinker's honesty is measured by his stance toward [Nietzsche](../philosophers/nietzsche.md) and Marx, transposes the genealogical suspicion into sociology: his analysis of values, of ressentiment and of the "disenchantment of the world" owes much to the Nietzschean diagnosis.

## Influence on

- **[Frankfurt School](../movements/frankfurt-school.md)** (reinterprets) : The rationalization [Weber](../philosophers/weber.md) described as the fate of the West becomes, in [Horkheimer](../philosophers/horkheimer.md) and [Adorno](../philosophers/adorno.md), [instrumental reason](../glossary/raison-instrumentale.md): no longer a fact to be recorded but a domination to be criticized. [Habermas](../philosophers/habermas.md) again takes up Weber to set instrumental action against communicative action.
