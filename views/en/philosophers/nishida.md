# Nishida Kitarō

*1870 – 1945 · Japanese · [Kyoto School](../movements/kyoto-school.md)*

Founder of the [Kyoto School](../movements/kyoto-school.md), thinker of [pure experience](../glossary/pure-experience.md) and of the "place" of absolute nothingness, between Zen and Western philosophy.
