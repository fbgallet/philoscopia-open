# Dilthey

*1833 – 1911 · German · [Lebensphilosophie](../movements/life-philosophy.md)*

German philosopher who sought to ground the human sciences in lived experience: nature is explained, psychic and historical life is understood.

## Influence on

- **[Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md)** (reinterprets) : [Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md) takes from [Dilthey](../philosophers/dilthey.md) the question of historical life and its understanding, to which he devotes a section of *[Being and Time](../works/being-and-time-heidegger.md)*. He reproaches him for not having raised the question of the being of that life.
- **[Philosophical hermeneutics](../movements/hermeneutics.md)** (reinterprets) : [Dilthey](../philosophers/dilthey.md) had made understanding the proper method of the human sciences. [Gadamer](../philosophers/gadamer.md) credits him with that question but reproaches him for remaining captive to the natural sciences' ideal of objectivity: understanding is not a method but our belonging to history.
