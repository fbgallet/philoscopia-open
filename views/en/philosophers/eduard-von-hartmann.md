# Eduard von Hartmann

*1842 – 1906 · German*

German philosopher whose Philosophy of the Unconscious sought to reconcile [Schopenhauer](../philosophers/schopenhauer.md) and [Hegel](../philosophers/hegel.md): an unconscious at work in history, in a world where the sum of pains outweighs the sum of joys. His book set off the pessimism controversy.
