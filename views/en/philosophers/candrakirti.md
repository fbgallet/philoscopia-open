# Candrakīrti

*600 – 650 · Indian · [Madhyamaka](../movements/madhyamaka.md)*

Commentator on [Nāgārjuna](../philosophers/nagarjuna.md), the reference for the "Prāsaṅgika" reading of [Madhyamaka](../movements/madhyamaka.md), which proceeds by reductio without a thesis of its own.

## Influenced by

- **[Yogācāra](../movements/yogacara.md)** (reacts against) : In the Madhyamakāvatāra, [Candrakīrti](../philosophers/candrakirti.md) refutes the [Yogācāra](../movements/yogacara.md) thesis at length: if one denies the external object, one must also deny a consciousness that would exist by itself. This debate fixed, in Tibet, the hierarchy of the schools.
