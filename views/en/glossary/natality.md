# Natality

*Concept associated with [Hannah Arendt](../philosophers/arendt.md)*

A concept of [Hannah Arendt](../philosophers/arendt.md)'s (The Human Condition, 1958). Each birth brings into the world someone new, able to begin something nothing allowed one to foresee. Natality is therefore not first a biological fact: it is the condition of action, the human capacity to start something new. Arendt sees in it what saves the world from wear and repetition, and reads in the Gospels' announcement, "a child has been born unto us", the expression of the faith and hope one can have in the world.
