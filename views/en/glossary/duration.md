# Duration (Bergsonian)

*Concept associated with [Bergson](../philosophers/bergson.md)*

In [Bergson](../philosophers/bergson.md), the lived time of consciousness: a continuity of change in which moments melt into one another like the notes of a melody. It stands against clock time, which cuts it up into instants set side by side like points in space.
