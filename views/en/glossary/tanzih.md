# Tanzīh (God's transcendence)

In Islamic theology, tanzīh means affirming that God is free of the limitations, imperfections and likenesses proper to creatures. The Muʿtazilites stress divine unity in particular, refusing to posit eternal attributes really distinct from the essence, without denying that God is knowing or powerful. Their opponents may reproach them for emptying the attributes of their reality. The term is opposed to tashbīh, the likening of God to creatures.
