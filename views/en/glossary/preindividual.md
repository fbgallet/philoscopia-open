# Preindividual

*Concept associated with [Gilbert Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md)*

In [Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md), the state of being before any [individuation](../glossary/individuation.md): not an individual in embryo nor a formless matter, but a reality rich in potentials and tensions, more than one and more than identity. Every individuation resolves part of it, and the individual keeps with it a charge of the preindividual, the source of its later individuations.
