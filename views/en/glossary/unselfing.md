# Unselfing

*Concept associated with [Murdoch](../philosophers/iris-murdoch.md)*

[Iris Murdoch](../philosophers/iris-murdoch.md)'s term for the movement by which attention detaches from the self's preoccupations and turns to what exists outside it. Her example is the kestrel: absorbed in resentment, I suddenly see a bird hovering, and the injured self disappears. It is neither a withdrawal from the world, nor sacrifice, nor mortification, but a loosening of egocentrism that makes one, on the contrary, more present to the world; the beauty of nature, great art and learning a demanding discipline are its occasions.
