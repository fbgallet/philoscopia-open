# Dignity of man

A Renaissance theme championed by [Pico della Mirandola](../philosophers/pico.md): the human being is the only creature without a fixed place, free to give itself its form. The idea of dignity is later taken up differently in [Kant](../philosophers/kant.md), as the unconditional worth of the person.
