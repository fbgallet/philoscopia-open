# Attention

In the ordinary sense, the capacity to focus one's mind on an object. The word takes on a moral sense in [Simone Weil](../philosophers/simone-weil.md): attention is a waiting, a way of suspending one's thought so as to let the object, or the person in affliction, appear as it is; she makes it the substance of prayer and of love of neighbour. [Iris Murdoch](../philosophers/iris-murdoch.md) takes up the term and secularizes it: attention is a just and loving gaze directed on an individual reality, the characteristic activity of the moral agent, which prepares choices long before they arise.
