# Supervenience

Two things cannot differ at the upper level without differing at the lower one: if two brains are physically identical down to the last atom, they cannot differ in what they undergo. Supervenience thus states a strict dependence without stating an identity, as a painting's beauty supervenes on the arrangement of its pigments without being that arrangement. It is the tool that lets one hold together "everything depends on the physical" and "not everything reduces to the physical", and it is the point at which non-reductionists are asked what still distinguishes their position from [materialism](../glossary/materialism.md).
