# Care (Sorge)

*Concept associated with [Martin Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md)*

In [Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md), care is neither psychological worry nor moral solicitude: it is the name of the being of [Dasein](../glossary/dasein.md) taken as a whole. To exist is always already to be ahead of oneself (projected toward one's possibilities), already in a world (thrown into a situation one did not choose) and alongside the things one is concerned with. Being and Time then shows that the meaning of this structure is temporality. Not to be confused with the [care](../glossary/care-ethics.md) of contemporary ethics, which means looking after others.
