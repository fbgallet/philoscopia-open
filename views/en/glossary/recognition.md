# Recognition (social)

A concept that takes its decisive form in [Hegel](../philosophers/hegel.md), after Fichte: one becomes oneself only through being recognized by others. Axel Honneth turns it into a social theory, in which disrespect (love withheld, rights denied, esteem withdrawn) motivates struggles for recognition.
