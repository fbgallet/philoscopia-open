# Individuation (Simondon)

*Concept associated with [Gilbert Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md)*

In [Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md), the operation by which an individual forms out of a system charged with potentials, together with its milieu. Rather than seeking, as the tradition did, a principle that would explain an individual assumed to be already given (the [principle of individuation](../glossary/principium-individuationis.md)), one must know the individual through individuation. It occurs in several regimes, physical (the crystal), vital, psychic and collective, and in the living being it continues throughout existence.
