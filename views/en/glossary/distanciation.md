# Distanciation

*Concept associated with [Ricœur](../philosophers/ricoeur.md)*

In [Ricœur](../philosophers/ricoeur.md), the autonomy a text acquires once written, with respect to its author's intention, situation and first audience. Far from being an obstacle, it makes possible both the explanation of the text and its appropriation by other readers.
