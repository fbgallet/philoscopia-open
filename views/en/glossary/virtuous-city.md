# Virtuous city

*Concept associated with [al-Fārābī](../philosophers/al-farabi.md)*

For [al-Fārābī](../philosophers/al-farabi.md), the virtuous city is one whose inhabitants cooperate towards true happiness, under a leadership able to bring them there. Its first, ideal ruler combines the capacities of philosopher and prophet: he knows the truth through the intellect and can convey it through images, precepts and laws. Inspired notably by [Plato](../philosophers/plato.md)'s Republic, it stands against the ignorant cities, which mistake wealth, honours or pleasure for happiness. Its leadership can then pass on in various ways, including to a council.
