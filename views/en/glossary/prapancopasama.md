# Prapañcopaśama (stilling of conceptual proliferation)

For [Nāgārjuna](../philosophers/nagarjuna.md), prapañcopaśama is the stilling of the conceptual proliferation through which we ascribe to things an independent [intrinsic nature](../glossary/svabhava.md). The verses of homage of the Fundamental Verses on the Middle Way link this stilling to the [dependent origination](../glossary/pratityasamutpada.md) taught by the Buddha. The point is neither to replace a supposed essence with a new absolute reality, nor to abolish all conventional language. The theme carries on a Buddhist heritage older than Nāgārjuna.
