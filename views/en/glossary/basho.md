# Place (basho)

*Concept associated with [Nishida Kitarō](../philosophers/nishida.md)*

For [Nishida](../philosophers/nishida.md), "place" (basho) is that in which something is and can be thought: an encompassing field rather than a subject or an object. Nishida distinguishes levels of it, from the place of being to the place of relative nothingness, up to the place of [absolute nothingness](../glossary/absolute-nothingness.md), which is no longer contained in anything. Drawing notably on the chōra of [Plato](../philosophers/plato.md)'s Timaeus, this "logic of place" seeks to think what the logic of the subject inherited from [Aristotle](../philosophers/aristotle.md) lets slip.
