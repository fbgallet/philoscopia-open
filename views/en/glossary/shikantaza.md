# Shikantaza ("just sitting")

*Concept associated with [Dōgen](../philosophers/dogen.md)*

The sitting advocated by [Dōgen](../philosophers/dogen.md): sitting with no object of meditation and no goal to reach, letting thoughts come and go. For him, this sitting is not a means of obtaining awakening but its expression.
