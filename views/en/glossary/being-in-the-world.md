# Being-in-the-world

*Concept associated with [Martin Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md)*

The basic structure of [Dasein](../glossary/dasein.md) according to [Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md): existence is not a subject shut up in itself that would then try to reach an outer world; it is from the outset alongside things, caught up in a web of uses, references and concerns. The hammer is understood in hammering, before any theory. The notion rejects the Cartesian split between subject and object, which it treats as a late abstraction. [Merleau-Ponty](../philosophers/merleau-ponty.md) takes it up again, giving it a bodily anchoring.
