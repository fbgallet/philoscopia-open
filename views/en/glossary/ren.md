# Ren (humaneness, benevolence)

Rén (仁), often translated as "humaneness" or "benevolence", is a central virtue of [Confucianism](../movements/confucianism.md), engaging the moral quality of our relations with others. For [Confucius](../philosophers/confucius.md), it is cultivated notably in family relations and fulfilled through a right practice of the rites. Its reach goes beyond those closest to us, without erasing the differences of responsibility towards each. Some Neo-Confucians, notably Cheng Hao and [Wang Yangming](../philosophers/wang-yangming.md), describe it as the capacity to form "one body" with the beings of the world.
