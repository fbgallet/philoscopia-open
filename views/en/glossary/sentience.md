# Sentience

Sentience is the capacity to feel sensations and affective states, pleasure, pain, fear, and so to have a point of view on what happens to one. For pathocentrism, it is the criterion that brings a being into the moral circle. Its boundary is still debated: it is ascribed to mammals and birds, increasingly to fish, octopuses and some crustaceans, and the question remains open for insects.
