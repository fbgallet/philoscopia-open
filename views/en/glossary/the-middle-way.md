# The Middle Way

*Concept associated with [The Buddha](../philosophers/buddha.md)*

The Middle Way is the course [the Buddha](../philosophers/buddha.md) charts between two extremes: the unbridled pursuit of sensual pleasures and ascetic self-mortification, both of which he had tried without finding liberation. It takes concrete form in the [Noble Eightfold Path](../glossary/eightfold-path.md). Later, the tradition broadened the formula to a refusal of metaphysical extremes, between eternalism and annihilationism. For [Nāgārjuna](../philosophers/nagarjuna.md), the middle way also carries on the Buddhist teaching that avoids the extremes of existence and non-existence: it refuses to ascribe to things an independent [intrinsic nature](../glossary/svabhava.md), without denying their conventional existence and efficacy. This approach, at once philosophical and liberating, gives its name to the [Madhyamaka](../movements/madhyamaka.md) school.
