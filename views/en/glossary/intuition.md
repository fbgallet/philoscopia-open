# Intuition (Bergsonian)

*Concept associated with [Bergson](../philosophers/bergson.md)*

In [Bergson](../philosophers/bergson.md), a knowledge that grasps its object from within, by sympathizing with its movement, instead of breaking it down into concepts as the intellect does. It is the method he assigns to metaphysics.
