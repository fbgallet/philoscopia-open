# Qi (vital force)

In Chinese thought, qì (氣) is the energy-matter of which all things are made, more or less dense or subtle, clear or turbid. [Neo-Confucianism](../movements/neo-confucianism.md) pairs it with [principle](../glossary/li-principle.md): qi gives things their concrete reality, li their form and their norm. For [Zhu Xi](../philosophers/zhu-xi.md), a turbid qi explains how the goodness of human nature can be obscured.
