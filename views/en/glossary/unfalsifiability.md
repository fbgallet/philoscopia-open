# Unfalsifiability

A theory is unfalsifiable when no possible observation could contradict it. For [Popper](../philosophers/popper.md), this is a flaw, not a strength: a scientific theory exposes itself to refutation, it says what should not happen if it is true. An explanation that accommodates everything, even its opposite, says nothing about the world.
