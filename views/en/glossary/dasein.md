# Dasein

*Concept associated with [Martin Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md)*

An ordinary German word meaning "existence" (literally "being-there"), to which [Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md) gives a technical use: it names the being that we ourselves are, the one for whom, in its being, that very being is at issue. The term deliberately avoids "man", "subject" or "consciousness", too laden with the metaphysics he wants to undo: Dasein is not a thing endowed with properties, but a way of existing, always already thrown into a world and open to the question of being. Most translators leave it in German.
