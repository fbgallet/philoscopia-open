# Extrinsic validity (parataḥ prāmāṇya)

In [Nyāya](../movements/nyaya.md), the validity of a cognition depends on conditions that do not reduce to the mere production of that cognition, and it is established by a distinct cognition, notably an inference from the success of action. Mīmāṃsā defends, on the contrary, an intrinsic validity (svataḥ prāmāṇya): a cognition needs no outside confirmation to carry authority, as long as no invalidating factor turns up. The debate concerns the conditions under which validity is produced and recognized. It does not, then, simply oppose a duty to check everything to a duty to believe without examination.
