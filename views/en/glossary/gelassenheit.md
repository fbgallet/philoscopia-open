# Releasement (Gelassenheit)

*Concept associated with [Martin Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md)*

A word from Rhineland mysticism (Meister Eckhart), where it names the detachment by which the soul lets itself go to God, taken up and transformed by [Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md) in 1955. Gelassenheit is no longer a religious surrender but an attitude of non-willing toward technology: saying both "yes" to the use of technical objects and "no" to their claim to rule over our being, while remaining open to the meaning that withdraws in them. English renderings include "releasement" and "composure".
