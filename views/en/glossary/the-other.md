# The Other (in Beauvoir)

*Concept associated with [Beauvoir](../philosophers/beauvoir.md)*

In [Beauvoir](../philosophers/beauvoir.md), the position in which man has placed woman: he has posited himself as the subject, the essential, and defined her in relation to himself, as the inessential. *[The Second Sex](../works/the-second-sex-beauvoir.md)* describes how this otherness was constructed and internalized.
