# Multiple realizability

One and the same mental function can be realized by very different substrates: pain, in a human, an octopus or a machine, would not need the same material to be pain, provided the role is played, namely being caused by injury, prompting flight, being learned from. Hilary Putnam made this, in the 1960s, the central argument against the idea that a mental state is identical with one determinate brain state, and the foundation of [functionalism](../axes/machine-mind.md). How far this indifference to substrate extends remains open: pushed to the limit, the same argument grants thought to a crowd of people passing messages.
