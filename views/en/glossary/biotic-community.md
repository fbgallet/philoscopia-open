# Biotic community

The biotic community, for [Aldo Leopold](../philosophers/leopold.md) ("A Sand County Almanac", 1949), is the interdependent whole of soils, waters, plants and animals in a place, of which humans are members, not masters. His "land ethic" extends morality to this community: a thing is right when it tends to preserve its integrity, stability and beauty.
