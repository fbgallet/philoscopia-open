# Capability

*Concept associated with [Sen](../philosophers/sen.md)*

Brought to the center of political philosophy by the economist and philosopher [Amartya Sen](../philosophers/sen.md), a capability is what a person can actually be and do: not their resources alone, nor their rights alone, but the lives they can really choose given their health, education, and situation. Two people with equal rights and equal incomes do not have the same capabilities if illness or discrimination closes to one what remains open to the other. Taken up by [Martha Nussbaum](../philosophers/nussbaum.md) in her own version, the notion serves to measure freedom and development by [effective possibilities](../axes/political-liberty.md) rather than formal permissions.
