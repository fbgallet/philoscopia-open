# Buddha nature

In Mahāyāna Buddhism, the capacity for awakening present in all beings. Chan makes it the core of its teaching; [Dōgen](../philosophers/dogen.md) refuses to see it as a hidden substance and reads: all beings are Buddha nature, and this nature is [impermanence](../glossary/anicca.md) itself.
