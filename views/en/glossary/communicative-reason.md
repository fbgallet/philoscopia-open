# Communicative reason

*Concept associated with [Habermas](../philosophers/habermas.md)*

For [Habermas](../philosophers/habermas.md), rationality oriented towards mutual understanding: interlocutors raise validity claims (truth, rightness, sincerity) that can be challenged and must then be justified. Explicit argument develops this rationality without exhausting it. Habermas does not reject reasoning about means; he refuses to reduce all [rationality](../glossary/raison-instrumentale.md) to it, and communicative reason makes it possible to criticize the social relations that prevent free discussion.
