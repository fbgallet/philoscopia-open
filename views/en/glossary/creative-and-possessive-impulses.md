# Creative and possessive impulses

*Concept associated with [Russell](../philosophers/russell.md)*

A division [Russell](../philosophers/russell.md) proposes in order to describe human beings without judging them good or bad. Possessive impulses bear on goods only one person can have: land, money, rank, power — what I obtain, another loses. Creative impulses bear on goods that are not lost by being shared: knowledge, art, affection, discovery. Neither is more natural than the other; institutions decide which one grows, and a politics is judged by the one it feeds. Hence a [plasticity without optimism](../axes/human-vision.md): human beings are neither corrupted nor saved by society, they are shaped, and nothing guarantees it will be for the better.
