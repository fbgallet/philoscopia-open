# Li (principle)

In [Neo-Confucianism](../movements/neo-confucianism.md), li is the intelligible and normative structure of things, what makes it possible to understand how they are constituted and how they are rightly fulfilled. For [Zhu Xi](../philosophers/zhu-xi.md), principle is one but its manifestations are differentiated, and it is met in things only in relation to [qi](../glossary/qi.md), the vital force that makes them up concretely. Self-cultivation aims to let heavenly principle, tianli, express itself rather than be obscured by selfish desires. This lǐ (理) is to be confused neither with the rites, lǐ (禮), nor with the benefit dear to the Mohists, lì (利).
