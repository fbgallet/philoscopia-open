# Metabolism

In biology, the set of exchanges of matter and energy by which an organism maintains itself. [Jonas](../philosophers/jonas.md) makes it the starting point of a philosophy of life: the organism persists only by constantly renewing its matter, so it has an identity of form distinct from its constituents, an interest in its own being and a first form of freedom, a "needful freedom", since it is independent of any particular matter yet depends on the world to find it. That, for him, is where inwardness and purposiveness begin.
