# Graded love

In the [Confucian](../movements/confucianism.md) tradition, the idea that affection and duties extend in circles, from those near to those far: we owe more to our parents than to strangers, without strangers being excluded. [Mencius](../philosophers/mencius.md) defends it against the [care for all](../glossary/jian-ai.md) preached by [Mozi](../philosophers/mozi.md): loving everyone in the same way would mean no longer honouring one's father. Contemporary debates on [partiality in ethics](../axes/partiality.md) still echo it.
