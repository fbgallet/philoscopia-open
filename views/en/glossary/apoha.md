# Apoha (exclusion)

For the Buddhist logicians Dignāga and Dharmakīrti, apoha explains the meaning of words and concepts by the exclusion of what is other. The concept "cow" thus marks out its domain by excluding non-cows, rather than by grasping a universal essence really present in each animal. The theory aims to account for classification without admitting [real universals](../glossary/realism-of-universals.md). [Nyāya](../movements/nyaya.md) counters it notably with the view that universals exist and can be perceived.
