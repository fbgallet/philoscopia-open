# Occasionalism

Occasionalism holds that created things have no autonomous causal power: God produces the effect on the occasion of what we call its cause. In an important Ashʿarite tradition, then, it is not fire that burns cotton, but God who creates the burning when the two meet. [Al-Ghazālī](../philosophers/al-ghazali.md) criticizes the necessity of the causal link, but reading his position as strict occasionalism remains debated. An occasionalist doctrine also develops in Malebranche in the seventeenth century, while the comparison with [Hume](../philosophers/hume.md) bears on the critique of causal necessity, not on any shared theology.
