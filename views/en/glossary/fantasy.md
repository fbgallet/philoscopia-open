# Fantasy (in Murdoch)

*Concept associated with [Murdoch](../philosophers/iris-murdoch.md)*

For [Iris Murdoch](../philosophers/iris-murdoch.md), the activity by which the self produces consoling or flattering images of the world and of others to protect itself from what is. She contrasts it with imagination, which on the contrary serves to see reality more justly. Fantasy is for her the chief enemy of the moral life, more than ill will, and the criterion that separates mediocre art, which consoles, from great art, which reveals.
