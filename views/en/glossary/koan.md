# Kōan

An exemplary case, often a dialogue, a question or a master's saying, used in the teaching and practice of [Chan and Zen](../movements/chan-zen.md); the Chinese *gong'an* means "public case". It is frequently paradoxical, though not always, and serves as the focus of sustained inquiry, tested in interviews with the teacher, aimed at awakening: "What is the sound of one hand?", from the eighteenth-century Japanese master [Hakuin](../philosophers/hakuin.md). The kōan collections took shape in China under the Song and lie at the heart of Rinzai Zen.
