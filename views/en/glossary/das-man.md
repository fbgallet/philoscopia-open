# The "They" (das Man)

*Concept associated with [Martin Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md)*

In [Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md), the anonymous subject of everyday life: one thinks what one thinks, does what is done, one dies, but never I. The "they" is nobody in particular; it relieves each of us of responsibility for our existence and levels it down in idle talk, curiosity and ambiguity. It is not a moral failing of a few, but a way of being of every [Dasein](../glossary/dasein.md), from which only [authentic](../glossary/authenticity.md) existence recovers itself, notably in the face of death.
