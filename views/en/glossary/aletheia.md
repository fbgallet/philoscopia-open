# Aletheia

The ordinary Greek word for "truth". Formed from the privative prefix a- and lēthē, oblivion or concealment, it was reread by [Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md) as "unconcealment": before being the agreement of a statement with a thing, truth would be the disclosure through which a being emerges from concealment and shows itself. This reading makes [truth as correspondence](../axes/truth-nature.md) a derivative form. Classicists dispute it, and Heidegger himself later admitted that aletheia so understood is not yet truth in the proper sense.
