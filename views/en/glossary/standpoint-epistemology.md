# Standpoint epistemologies

An approach within feminist social epistemology, with extensions into philosophy of science and political theory (Sandra Harding, Nancy Hartsock). It holds that a critical standpoint, worked out from a subordinated social position, can reveal what the dominant position conceals: for instance, the domestic work invisible in economic models. This standpoint is a collective achievement, not an automatic privilege of lived circumstances.
