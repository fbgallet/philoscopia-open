# Dhawq (taste, lived knowledge)

Literally "taste", dhawq means, in [al-Ghazālī](../philosophers/al-ghazali.md) and in Sufism, a knowledge gained by directly undergoing a spiritual state. Being able to define drunkenness is not being drunk: likewise, describing a religious experience is not living it. For the lived knowledge of spiritual realities, al-Ghazālī stresses what such experience brings beyond discourse and demonstration. This does not mean that he gives up reason, nor that he replaces all rational knowledge with mystical experience.
