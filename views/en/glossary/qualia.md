# Qualia

Qualia are the lived qualities of an experience: red as one sees it, pain as it hurts, the taste of coffee. The word names "what it is like" to be in a mental state, as opposed to its physical description. The whole difficulty is that they seem accessible to one person only: one can measure a wavelength, not the red someone sees. For some they are what no science of the brain will ever reach, marking the limits of [materialism](../glossary/materialism.md); for others, an illusion introspection makes about itself.
