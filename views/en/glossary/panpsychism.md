# Panpsychism

Panpsychism holds that a form of inwardness is a fundamental property of matter, present everywhere in vanishing degrees, and of which our consciousness would be the most elaborate form. It does not ascribe thought to every pebble: the elementary experience one would attribute to an electron would be nothing like a point of view on the world. It is the radical version of [dual-aspect monism](../axes/consciousness-nature.md): where the latter says only that physics describes from outside what experience gives from within, panpsychism extends that inner face to all matter. Its own obstacle is the combination problem: how would myriad micro-experiences make a single subject?
