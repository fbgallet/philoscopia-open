# Enframing (Gestell)

*Concept associated with [Martin Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md)*

The name [Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md) gives to the essence of modern technology. In ordinary German, **Gestell** means a rack or a frame; he makes it the gathering of all the ways of "setting" (stellen): challenging, ordering, requisitioning. Modern technology no longer lets things come forth as the craftsman or farmer of old did; it challenges them to yield their energy and to stand by as **standing-reserve** (Bestand): the river for the power plant, the forest for cellulose, the human being itself as a resource. The danger is not first the machine, but that this mode of revealing closes off every other relation to being. Translations vary: "enframing", "Ge-stell", "positionality".
