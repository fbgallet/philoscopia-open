# Logical atomism

*Concept associated with [Russell](../philosophers/russell.md)*

The doctrine that the world is a plurality of independent facts, and not a whole from which each part would draw its nature. Under analysis a complex fact decomposes into atomic facts — an object has such a quality, two objects stand in such a relation — matched by elementary propositions; compound propositions reduce to these by the operations of logic. [Russell](../philosophers/russell.md) coined the term in 1911 and developed it in 1918, against the [holism](../axes/individual-society.md) of the idealists he had followed in his youth: atomism is first of all a refusal, that of the thesis that nothing is intelligible outside the whole. [Wittgenstein](../philosophers/wittgenstein.md) gives a more radical version of it in the Tractatus, then turns against it.
