# Fusion of horizons

*Concept associated with [Gadamer](../philosophers/gadamer.md)*

In [Gadamer](../philosophers/gadamer.md), what happens when one understands a text or an era: the horizon of the present widens by taking in that of the past, into a single, wider horizon in which neither present nor past simply prevails.
