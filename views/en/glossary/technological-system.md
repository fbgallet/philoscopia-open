# Technological system

*Concept associated with [Jacques Ellul](../philosophers/ellul.md)*

A notion of [Jacques Ellul](../philosophers/ellul.md)'s: technique is not a set of tools but the ensemble of methods pursuing maximal efficiency in every field. Linked together, notably by computing, techniques form a system that has become humanity's milieu in place of nature. The system is called **autonomous** in a precise sense: it grows according to its own logic of efficiency, and political, economic and moral institutions tend to conform to it more than they steer it. Ellul does not conclude that all resistance is futile, but that it begins by desacralizing technique.
