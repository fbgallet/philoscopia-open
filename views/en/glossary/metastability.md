# Metastability

*Concept associated with [Gilbert Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md)*

A notion borrowed from physics: a state is metastable when it appears stable but holds a potential energy that a slight disturbance can release, like a supersaturated solution in which a seed triggers crystallization. [Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md) makes it the regime of [preindividual](../glossary/preindividual.md) being: neither stable nor merely unstable, but charged with potentials that [individuation](../glossary/individuation.md) resolves.
