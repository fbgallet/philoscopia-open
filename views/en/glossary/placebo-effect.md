# Placebo effect

The placebo effect is the improvement produced by a treatment with no active ingredient, through expectation, the ritual of care, the relationship with the caregiver. It is real, especially for pain, anxiety or fatigue, but it does not shrink a tumour or cure an infection. That is why a remedy is judged effective only if it does better than a placebo, in trials where neither patient nor doctor knows who receives what.
