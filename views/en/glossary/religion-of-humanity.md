# Religion of Humanity

*Concept associated with [Comte](../philosophers/comte.md)*

A religion without God developed by [Comte](../philosophers/comte.md) late in life, to unite people in the service of Humanity and cultivate altruism. Its object is the Great Being: not the mere totality of humans, but those, dead, living and to come, whose contribution sustains collective life. It has its calendar, its secular saints and its motto: “love as principle, order as basis, progress as goal”.
