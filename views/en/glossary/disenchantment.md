# Disenchantment of the world

The disenchantment of the world refers, in [Max Weber](../philosophers/weber.md) (lecture "Science as a Vocation", 1917), to the retreat of mysterious, incalculable forces before the idea that everything can, in principle, be mastered by calculation. Science gained an intelligible world from it; many lost a world inhabited by meaning. The success of astrology or "energies" can be understood partly as a desire for re-enchantment.
