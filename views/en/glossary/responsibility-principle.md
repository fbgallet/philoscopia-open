# Imperative of responsibility

*Concept associated with [Hans Jonas](../philosophers/jonas.md)*

The title of [Jonas](../philosophers/jonas.md)'s book (1979) and the name of the doctrine he defends in it. Because technology gives our acts cumulative, irreversible and planetary effects, ethics must extend to [future generations](../axes/future-generations.md). Jonas reformulates [Kant](../philosophers/kant.md)'s imperative: "Act so that the effects of your action are compatible with the permanence of genuine human life on Earth." This responsibility rests not on reciprocity but on the vulnerability of what depends on us, on the model of parents toward a newborn.
