# Narrative identity

*Concept associated with [Ricœur](../philosophers/ricoeur.md)*

In [Ricœur](../philosophers/ricoeur.md), the understanding of oneself that passes through the stories that give a life its shape, those one receives as well as those one tells. It mediates between sameness (*idem*), whose privileged expression is character, and selfhood (*ipse*), whose model is the promise kept. Someone can thus change profoundly and remain the one who answers for their commitments.
