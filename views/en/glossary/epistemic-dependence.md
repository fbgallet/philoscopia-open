# Epistemic dependence

Epistemic dependence is the fact that most of what we know comes to us through other people's testimony, which we cannot check ourselves: textbooks, experts, instruments, people close to us. The philosopher John Hardwig (1985) showed that it is not a weakness but the condition of a knowledge too vast for a single mind. The question is therefore not how to escape it, but whom to trust, and why.
