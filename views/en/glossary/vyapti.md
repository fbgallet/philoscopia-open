# Vyāpti (invariable concomitance)

In Indian logic, vyāpti is an invariable relation that licenses inference: wherever there is smoke there is fire, without the converse being necessarily true. [Nyāya](../movements/nyaya.md) seeks to establish it by examining relevant cases, notably fire present with smoke and smoke absent where fire is lacking. One must also rule out restricting conditions, called upādhi, and resort to hypothetical reasoning, tarka. This inquiry meets difficulties comparable to those of the problem of induction, without being the same problem.
