# Critical theory

*Concept associated with [Horkheimer](../philosophers/horkheimer.md)*

The programme set out by [Horkheimer](../philosophers/horkheimer.md) in 1937: a theory of society that knows itself to be part of what it studies and aims at emancipation, against "traditional" theory, which believes itself neutral. The name has since designated the tradition of the [Frankfurt School](../movements/frankfurt-school.md).
