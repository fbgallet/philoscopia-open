# Gewu (investigation of things)

Géwù (格物), a phrase from the Great Learning read in diverse ways, means for [Zhu Xi](../philosophers/zhu-xi.md) the investigation of things and affairs in order to understand their [principle](../glossary/li-principle.md). Through careful, cumulative study, this understanding is meant to reach an all-embracing insight. [Wang Yangming](../philosophers/wang-yangming.md) recounts a failed attempt in his youth to investigate bamboo, an episode he rereads in the light of his later doctrine. He then reinterprets gewu as a moral rectification of the affairs the mind applies itself to, rather than as a search for a principle outside it.
