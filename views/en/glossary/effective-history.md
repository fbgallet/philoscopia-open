# Effective history (Wirkungsgeschichte)

*Concept associated with [Gadamer](../philosophers/gadamer.md)*

The history of the effects a work has produced, which shapes both the work as it reaches us and the situation of its interpreter. "Historically effected consciousness" is, for [Gadamer](../philosophers/gadamer.md), the interpreter's always partial awareness of being shaped by what precedes them.
