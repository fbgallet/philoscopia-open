# Skandha (aggregates)

The five aggregates through which Buddhism analyses a person's experience are material form, sensations, perceptions, mental formations and consciousness. None of them is to be identified with a permanent self, and their combination does not make up such a self either: this is the teaching of [non-self](../glossary/anatta.md). A person can nonetheless be conventionally designated in dependence on these aggregates. [Madhyamaka](../movements/madhyamaka.md) also refuses to ascribe to them an independent [intrinsic nature](../glossary/svabhava.md).
