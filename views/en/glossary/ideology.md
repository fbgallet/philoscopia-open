# Ideology

In the broad sense, the set of ideas and representations of a society or group; in the critical sense used here, a system of representations that makes a social order seem natural. For [Marx](../philosophers/marx.md), it can legitimate domination by presenting historical social relations as eternal, without necessarily involving conscious deception. For [Althusser](../philosophers/althusser.md), it constitutes individuals as subjects through practices and institutions: this is interpellation, at once recognition and subjection.
