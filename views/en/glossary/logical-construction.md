# Logical construction

*Concept associated with [Russell](../philosophers/russell.md)*

The device by which a doubtful entity is defined as a class or series of already admitted elements, instead of being posited behind them. A physical object becomes a class of appearances, a point of space a class of overlapping events, a person a series of experiences bound by laws, a number a class of classes. [Russell](../philosophers/russell.md) makes it the supreme maxim of philosophical work: wherever possible, substitute constructions out of known entities for inferences to unknown entities. The device is reductionist out of economy rather than materialism: whatever cannot be so constructed is to be suspected, but the reduction aimed at is logical, not physical.
