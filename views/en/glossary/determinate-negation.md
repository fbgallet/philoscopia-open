# Determinate negation

In [Hegel](../philosophers/hegel.md), a negation that does not simply destroy what it negates but keeps some of its content and produces something new. [Adorno](../philosophers/adorno.md) turns it against every system: criticizing what is by showing what it lacks, without claiming to reconcile the whole.
