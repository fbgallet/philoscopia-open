# Pseudoscience

A pseudoscience borrows the appearance of science, its vocabulary, its titles, sometimes its instruments, without accepting its test: its claims are not exposed to tests that could refute them, or survive their failures through explanations added after the fact. [Popper](../philosophers/popper.md) made it the heart of the **demarcation problem**. The criterion is not how strange an idea is, but its refusal to be proven wrong.
