# Heuristics of fear

*Concept associated with [Hans Jonas](../philosophers/jonas.md)*

A method proposed by [Jonas](../philosophers/jonas.md) for guiding action when technology produces distant effects we cannot predict. We know sooner what we do not want than what we want: picturing the threat hanging over humanity or nature teaches us what deserves to be preserved. This fear is not a paralysing panic but a duty of knowledge, cultivated by the imagination; it comes with a decision rule that gives priority to the prophecy of doom over the prophecy of bliss when the stake is irreversible. It fed into the precautionary principle.
