# Indirect duties

Indirect duties are duties we have regarding a being without having them toward it. For [Kant](../philosophers/kant.md), we owe nothing to animals in themselves, but cruelty to them is wrong because it hardens the heart and makes us harsher toward people: the duty bears, at bottom, on our own humanity.
