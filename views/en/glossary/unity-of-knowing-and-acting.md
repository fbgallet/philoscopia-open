# Unity of knowing and acting

*Concept associated with [Wang Yangming](../philosophers/wang-yangming.md)*

[Wang Yangming](../philosophers/wang-yangming.md)'s thesis (zhī xíng hé yī, 知行合一): truly to know the good is already to do it. Whoever claims to know filial piety without practising it does not yet know it, just as seeing a beautiful colour and loving it are one and the same act. The formula stands against the idea that one should first study and only then act; it made Wang a reference for nineteenth-century Japanese reformers.
