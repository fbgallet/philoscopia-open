# Preambles of faith

*Concept associated with [Thomas Aquinas](../philosophers/aquinas.md)*

Truths that natural reason can demonstrate and that faith presupposes, such as God's existence or unity. For [Thomas Aquinas](../philosophers/aquinas.md), they are distinct from the articles of faith, such as the Trinity, which reason can defend but not prove.
