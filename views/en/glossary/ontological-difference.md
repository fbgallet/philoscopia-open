# Ontological difference

*Concept associated with [Martin Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md)*

In [Heidegger](../philosophers/heidegger.md), the difference between being and beings. A being is whatever is: a stone, a tool, a human, even God thought of as the highest being. Being is none of these beings, but what makes them be and show themselves. According to him, Western metaphysics has constantly thought being on the model of a being, as enduring presence, first cause or ground: this is what he calls the "oblivion of being".
