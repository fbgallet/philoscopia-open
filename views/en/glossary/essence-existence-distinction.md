# Distinction between essence and existence

*Concept associated with [Avicenna](../philosophers/avicenna.md)*

For [Avicenna](../philosophers/avicenna.md), the essence of a possible being, what it is, is not enough to explain that it exists: one can understand what a triangle is without establishing that any given triangle exists. Its existence therefore depends on a cause, without its essence being an already existing thing to which existence would be added. This distinction plays a major role in Avicennian metaphysics. Received and transformed by [Thomas Aquinas](../philosophers/aquinas.md), who draws from it his doctrine of [esse](../glossary/esse.md), it became one of the pillars of scholastic metaphysics.
