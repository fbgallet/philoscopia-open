# Law of three stages

*Concept associated with [Comte](../philosophers/comte.md)*

A historical framework proposed by [Auguste Comte](../philosophers/comte.md) to describe the development of the human mind. Each domain of knowledge passes from the theological stage, which explains phenomena by supernatural agents, to the metaphysical stage, which explains them by abstract entities, and then to the positive stage, which gives up ultimate causes and seeks the laws governing phenomena. The sciences reach this stage at different times, sociology last.
