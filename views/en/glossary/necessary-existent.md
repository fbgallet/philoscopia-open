# Necessary Existent (wājib al-wujūd)

*Concept associated with [Avicenna](../philosophers/avicenna.md)*

For [Avicenna](../philosophers/avicenna.md), the Necessary Existent in itself is what cannot not exist and depends on no cause. All other beings are [possible in themselves](../glossary/essence-existence-distinction.md): their essence does not require their existence, even though that existence becomes necessary through the cause that makes them exist. Avicenna shows that possible beings depend, in the last analysis, on something necessary in itself, without assuming that the world began in time. This line of argument deeply influenced Latin [scholasticism](../glossary/scholasticism.md), which received and transformed it.
