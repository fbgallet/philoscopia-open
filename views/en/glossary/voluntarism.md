# Voluntarism (theological and moral)

A doctrine that gives the will primacy over the intellect in God; it often has as its counterpart a primacy of the will in human beings. The contrast with intellectualism is schematic: for [Thomas Aquinas](../philosophers/aquinas.md), God wills in accordance with his wisdom and goodness, without depending on any standard outside himself; for [Ockham](../philosophers/ockham.md), moral obligation rests on divine command, which God could have given otherwise, though this choice is not mere caprice.
