# Regression to the mean

Regression to the mean is the natural return of an extreme measurement toward its usual value. A pain, a fever, a bout of insomnia reaches a peak, then eases on its own; the remedy taken at the height of the trouble gets credit for an improvement that would have come anyway. It is one of the reasons why "it worked for me" does not prove a treatment works.
