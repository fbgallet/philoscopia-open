# Erlebnis (lived experience)

*Concept associated with [Dilthey](../philosophers/dilthey.md)*

Experience as it is lived from within, with its significance for the one living it. [Dilthey](../philosophers/dilthey.md) sees in it the material of the human sciences, which understand life rather than explain it; the term then passes into [phenomenology](../movements/phenomenology.md).
