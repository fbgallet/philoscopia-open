# Speciesism

Speciesism is giving less weight to a being's interests for the sole reason that it does not belong to our species. The term was coined by [Richard Ryder](../philosophers/ryder.md) in 1970 and popularized by [Peter Singer](../philosophers/singer.md), who compares it to racism: a discrimination based on a morally irrelevant trait. Its defenders reply that belonging to humanity is not a mere biological trait but membership in a community of persons.
