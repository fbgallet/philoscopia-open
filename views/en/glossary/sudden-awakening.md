# Sudden awakening

The thesis defended in the *[Platform Sutra](../works/platform-sutra-huineng.md)*, a text of Chinese [Chan](../movements/chan-zen.md): awakening is not the end point of a gradual accumulation but a direct seeing of one's own [nature](../glossary/buddha-nature.md), which is not a permanent self but the [emptiness](../glossary/sunyata.md) of the mind. This excludes neither preparation nor subsequent practice, and some traditions combine sudden awakening with gradual cultivation. The opposition between a "sudden" Southern school and a "gradual" Northern school is partly a polemical construction.
