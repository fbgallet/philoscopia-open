# Univocity of being

*Concept associated with [Duns Scotus](../philosophers/duns-scotus.md)*

[Duns Scotus](../philosophers/duns-scotus.md)'s thesis that the concept of being is said in the same sense of God and of creatures. Without this common concept, nothing could be known of God by reason. It stands against Thomist [analogy](../glossary/analogy-of-being.md).
