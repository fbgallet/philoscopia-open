# Burhān (demonstration)

In [falsafa](../movements/falsafa.md), burhān is reasoning that starts from certain premises and concludes necessarily, on the model of [Aristotle](../philosophers/aristotle.md)'s Posterior Analytics. The philosophers see it as the highest form of knowledge, above dialectic, which starts from accepted opinions, and rhetoric, which persuades. [Averroes](../philosophers/averroes.md) makes it the criterion for interpreting religious texts: where demonstration contradicts the apparent sense, the text must be read figuratively, by those competent to do so. The point is not to set two truths against each other, but to read the text at the level of demonstration.
