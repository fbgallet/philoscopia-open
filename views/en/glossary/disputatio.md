# Disputatio (the scholastic disputation)

A medieval university exercise in which a question is posed and then handled through a series of objections, an argument from authority to the contrary, a reply by the master and the refutation of each objection. The *quaestio*, its basic unit, still structures [Thomas Aquinas](../philosophers/aquinas.md)'s *[Summa Theologiae](../works/summa-theologiae-aquinas.md)*.
