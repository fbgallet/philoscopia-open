# Associated milieu

*Concept associated with [Gilbert Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md)*

In [Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md), the milieu that a concrete technical object creates around itself and which conditions it in return, at once technical and natural. In the Guimbal turbine, the river water drives the turbine and at the same time cools the alternator, which makes possible a device that would not have worked outside this coupling. It is not just any environment: it is a milieu the object brings into existence and on which its functioning depends.
