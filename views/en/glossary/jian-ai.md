# Jian ai (inclusive care for all)

Jiān ài (兼愛), inclusive care for all, is a central principle of [Mohism](../movements/mohism.md): it asks us to take into account other people, families and states, instead of exclusively favouring our own. Conflicts arise from this exclusive preference, while a care extended to all is justified by the benefits it brings. This does not necessarily imply identical feelings towards everyone, nor the abolition of family responsibilities. [Mencius](../philosophers/mencius.md) nonetheless sees in it a threat to the priority owed to one's father, and sets [graded love](../glossary/graded-love.md) against it.
