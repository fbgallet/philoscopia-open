# Transduction

*Concept associated with [Gilbert Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md)*

In [Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md), the operation by which a structure spreads step by step through a domain, each region already structured serving as the structuring principle for the next, like a crystal growing in its solution. It is not a synonym for transmission: it is both the mode of [individuation](../glossary/individuation.md) and the method of thought that follows it, without starting from already constituted individuals.
