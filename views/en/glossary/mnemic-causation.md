# Mnemic causation

*Concept associated with [Russell](../philosophers/russell.md)*

Russell's name for laws in which the present state of a being depends not on its immediately preceding state but on a past event that marked it: a memory, a habit, a conditioned reflex. The point of the notion is constructive: it allows a person to be defined as a series of events bound together by such laws — my memories depend on my past and not on yours — instead of positing a substantial self to carry them. [Logical construction](../glossary/logical-construction.md) thus replaces the subject. Russell grants that these laws might one day reduce to physical traces in the brain, which would change nothing in their role: reducible or not, they supply the principle of unity of a life.
