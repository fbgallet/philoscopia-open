# Negative way (apophatic theology)

A way of speaking of the first principle or of God that denies that properties drawn from finite beings can be applied to it literally: one says, for example, that God is not a body and is not in time. [Neoplatonism](../movements/neoplatonism.md) is a major source of it; Pseudo-Dionysius brought it into Christian theology, and [Maimonides](../philosophers/maimonides.md) developed it differently, admitting of God only negative attributes or attributes of his actions.
