# Barnum effect

The Barnum effect, or Forer effect, is the tendency to accept as an accurate description of oneself statements vague enough to fit almost anyone. The psychologist Bertram Forer demonstrated it in 1948 with a portrait drawn from horoscopes, which his students all judged accurate. It partly explains the success of horoscopes, fortune-telling and some personality tests.
