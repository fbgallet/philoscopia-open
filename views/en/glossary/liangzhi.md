# Liangzhi (innate moral knowledge)

[Wang Yangming](../philosophers/wang-yangming.md) takes over from [Mencius](../philosophers/mencius.md) liángzhī (良知), the moral knowledge everyone possesses without having acquired it through prior learning. This capacity to discern good from evil can be obscured by selfish desires and must be realized in actual conduct. "Extending liangzhi to the full" therefore means unfolding it in concrete situations, not holding every spontaneous conviction infallible. Wang makes it a central principle of self-cultivation, without being its inventor.
