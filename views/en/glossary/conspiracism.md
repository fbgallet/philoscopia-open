# Conspiracism

Conspiracism is the tendency to explain events by the deliberate, secret action of a small group manipulating the course of things for its own sole benefit. It differs from distrust of authorities, which responds to real errors or conflicts of interest and accepts being refuted, and from real conspiracies, which investigation has brought into the open. Its mark is being [unfalsifiable](../glossary/unfalsifiability.md): every denial becomes proof of the cover-up.
