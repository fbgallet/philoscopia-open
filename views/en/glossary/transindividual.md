# Transindividual

*Concept associated with [Gilbert Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md)*

In [Simondon](../philosophers/simondon.md), the dimension of psychic and collective [individuation](../glossary/individuation.md). The collective is neither a sum of ready-made individuals that would then enter into relation, nor a whole that would absorb them: it arises from the [preindividual](../glossary/preindividual.md) share each subject carries, which it can individuate only with others. Culture and technical objects are supports of this transindividual relation.
