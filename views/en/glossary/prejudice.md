# Prejudice (in the hermeneutic sense)

A judgment made before examination. The Enlightenment made it the enemy of reason; [Gadamer](../philosophers/gadamer.md) rehabilitates it as a condition of all understanding, since one never approaches a text from nowhere, while distinguishing fruitful prejudices from misleading ones.
