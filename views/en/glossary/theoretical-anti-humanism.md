# Theoretical anti-humanism

*Concept associated with [Althusser](../philosophers/althusser.md)*

[Althusser](../philosophers/althusser.md)'s thesis that history is explained not by a human essence or a sovereign constituting subject, but by social relations, practices and struggles. He speaks of a process without a subject, which does not eliminate individual agency. This is a theoretical claim, not a rejection of moral concern for people.
