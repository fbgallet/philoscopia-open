# Three natures (trisvabhāva)

A [Yogācāra](../movements/yogacara.md) doctrine distinguishing the imagined nature (things projected as subject and object), the dependent nature (the conditioned stream of consciousness) and the perfected nature (the dependent nature freed from this imagined duality).
