# Pramāṇa (means of valid knowledge)

In the philosophies of India, a pramāṇa is a means of valid knowledge, whose definition and criteria vary between schools. Classical [Nyāya](../movements/nyaya.md) recognizes four: perception, inference, comparison and reliable testimony; the Buddhist tradition of Dignāga and Dharmakīrti keeps perception and inference. Their number, scope and justification are a central stake of Indian controversies. [Nāgārjuna](../philosophers/nagarjuna.md) criticizes establishing them as independent foundations, without this requiring a denial of their conventional use.
