# Absolute nothingness

*Concept associated with [Nishida Kitarō](../philosophers/nishida.md)*

For [Nishida](../philosophers/nishida.md), absolute nothingness (zettai mu) is neither the absence of all being nor the opposite of being, but the non-objectifiable ["place"](../glossary/basho.md) within which their oppositions, and those of subject and object, can take shape. This place is not to be understood as a substance or a hidden world behind the world. Nishida works out this thought in dialogue with Buddhism, Zen in particular, and with Western philosophies. [Nishitani](../philosophers/nishitani.md) takes up and transforms the problem by making [emptiness](../glossary/sunyata.md) a way through modern nihilism.
