# Cathartic virtues

*Concept associated with [Porphyry](../philosophers/porphyry.md)*

In the hierarchy of virtues set out by [Porphyry](../philosophers/porphyry.md) after [Plotinus](../philosophers/plotinus.md), these are the virtues that purify the soul of its attachments to the body. They rank above the civic virtues, which merely regulate the passions, and prepare the contemplative virtues. Philosophy there becomes an exercise in detachment as much as a body of knowledge.
