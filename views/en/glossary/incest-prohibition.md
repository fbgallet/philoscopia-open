# Incest prohibition

A prohibition forbidding sexual relations and marriage between certain relatives; every known society has one, but they differ in which kinship ties it covers. For [Lévi-Strauss](../philosophers/levi-strauss.md), it is the reverse side of a positive rule: giving up the women of one's own group obliges one to exchange them with others, and these marriage alliances create reciprocity between groups. In his theory, the prohibition thus marks the passage from nature to culture.
